- This list is a comprehensive merge of permissions from:
  • Frontend React components (via hasAnyPermission, <Can />)
  • Backend controllers (via hasPermission, policies)
  • AdminLayout menu‑based permission checks
- Permissions with "bulk_", "force_", or "toggle" are used for batch or state‑change operations.
- The "*.manage" permissions are often catch‑all for full administrative access.
- Some modules use different prefixes (e.g., jobs vs job_listings, pages vs cms.pages)
  to separate frontend and backend contexts; both are included.
- Permissions like "get_active" are used for AJAX dropdown endpoints.
